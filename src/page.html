How it works

From first call to final cut

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    Tell us the date, the city and which houses you need. We confirm availability and set up a call.

  2. 02

    Recce & moodboard

    We visit the venue or kitchen, agree the look, the menu or the run of show, and share a written plan.

  3. 03

    Plan & quote

    Crew, equipment, timelines and deliverables go into one quote, so nothing is left to the day itself.

  4. 04

    The day

    Photographers, film crew, kitchen and AV arrive briefed and on time. You get one point of contact on the floor.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Galleries, films, albums, reels and reports arrive on the dates written in your quote.
