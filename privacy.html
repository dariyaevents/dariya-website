Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Dariya Events ("Dariya", "we", "us") is a wedding photography, cinematography and event company based in New Town, Kolkata, India. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you contact us — including through forms on Facebook and Instagram, our website, WhatsApp, phone or email — and how we use and protect it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number and email address.
  • Event details: your wedding or event date, location or venue, the services you are interested in, and the budget range you choose.
  • Messages: anything you tell us when you message, call or email us.
  • Ad and form information from Meta: when you submit a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, Meta shares your answers with us, along with which ad or form you responded to. Meta's own use of your data is covered by Meta's Privacy Policy.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your enquiry and understand your plans.
  • To prepare quotes, proposals and availability for your event.
  • To deliver and manage the services you book with us.
  • To keep records of enquiries and improve how we respond to them.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to your enquiry or booking.

Who we share it with

Your details are seen only by the Dariya team handling your enquiry. We use trusted tools to store and manage enquiries (for example, Meta's Leads Center, Google Sheets and WhatsApp Business). We may share information with service providers working on your event only where needed to deliver it, or where the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and follow up on your enquiry, and booking records for as long as required for our services, accounts and legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. We will act on your request within a reasonable time, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access, and limit access to people who need it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
